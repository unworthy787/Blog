There's a certain kind of courage required to put your thoughts on the internet. Not the big, dramatic kind — but the quiet courage of saying *this is what I think, right now, at this point in my life.*

I've been building things, learning things, and stumbling through things for years now. And for most of that time, I kept those experiences locked away in scattered notes, half-finished journal entries, and the unreliable storage of my own memory.

## The Turning Point

Something shifted when I realized that the posts I found most valuable online weren't the polished tutorials or the authoritative guides. They were the honest accounts of someone figuring something out — in real time, with all the wrong turns included.

I wanted to write *that kind* of content.

## What You'll Find Here

This blog is my attempt to document the journey. That means:

- **Technical explorations** — things I'm building, debugging, and learning
- **Ideas I'm wrestling with** — half-formed thoughts that deserve a full post
- **Life stuff** — because code doesn't exist in a vacuum

## A Note on Consistency

I'm not promising daily posts or weekly newsletters. I'm promising honesty. When I write something here, it'll be because I actually have something to say.

That feels like the right foundation.
